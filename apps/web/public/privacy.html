CaveCAD privacy

CaveCAD is a cave survey mapping app at cave.schonegg.stream, run by Nathan Schonegg. Contact: cavecad.app@gmail.com.

Your caves stay yours

Your caves live on your devices and in the drives you connect. CaveCAD's server never has your surveys, sketches, scans or locations in a form it can read.

For live sessions, the server keeps a locked history of edits so people can catch up. Only the people you share a cave with hold the key. The server, and its admins, can't open it.

What the server knows

  • Your name and email, from the Google account you sign in with.
  • The name and colour you choose to show others.
  • Which drives you connect (the kind of drive and its account name), never their keys.
  • Who invited you, your role, when you joined and when you were last seen.

It is used only to let you sign in and to run CaveCAD. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Signing in with Google

CaveCAD asks Google only for your name and email address. It does not see your Google password, and it does not read your Gmail, Drive or anything else in your Google account through this sign-in.

Leaving

Ask an admin, or write to cavecad.app@gmail.com, to have your account and everything the server knows about you removed. Your caves are on your own devices and drives and are not affected.
