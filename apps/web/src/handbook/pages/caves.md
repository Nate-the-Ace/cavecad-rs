# Caves: opening, starting and surface data

The Caves window lists your caves, the samples and the caves on your other devices; **New** starts a cave; **Surface Data** brings in what lies above it.

## The Caves window

- A cave **kept in this browser** lives only in this browser until you give it a folder (Home → Folder). Chrome, Edge and the desktop app can make the folder for you.
- A cave **on the CaveCAD server, from one of your other devices** is not on this computer yet. Open it to bring it onto this computer; it stays in step with your other devices.
- A **sample cave** is there to look around. Your first change makes it your own copy, kept in this browser; the sample stays as it is.

## Starting a new cave

- Without a folder, the new cave is kept in this browser. Home → Project gives you a folder of it later.
- After **Create**, you get your scans on the left and the notebook beside them. Pick the first page and type its shots as you read them. See [Bringing survey data in](bringing-survey-data-in) for other ways in.

## Surface Data

Surface Data is the aerial photo and the ground elevation above the cave, downloaded from USGS. They are kept with the cave and drawn under the [Map](map).
