# Sheet

The Sheet panel shows your cave map on a piece of paper at a chosen scale, with a border, title block, scale bar and north arrow, ready to print.

## When you reach for it

- You want a printed or PNG copy of the map.
- You want to check that the cave fits on the paper at a given scale.
- You want to add the cave's name and survey notes to a title block.

![The Sheet panel](sheet.png)

## How to use it

The Sheet panel only shows the page. You change it from the **Sheets** tab at the top of the window.

1. Open the **Sheet** panel to see the page. Opening the **Sheets** tab opens it for you.
2. In **Paper and scale**, pick the **Paper** size.
3. Pick the **Way up**: **Best way up**, **Landscape** or **Portrait**.
4. Pick a **Scale**. **Scale: best fit** chooses the largest scale that fits the cave on the paper.
5. In **Title block**, type the cave's name in **Cave name**, and in **Title block notes** type lines such as location, surveyors and date, separated by semicolons.
6. In **Show**, tick or untick **Title block**, **Scale bar** and **North**.
7. Read the note at the lower left of the panel. It says "Plotted at" followed by the scale.
8. In **Save**, click **PNG** to save a picture, or **Print** to open the print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" there for a PDF.

### Notes on paper and units

- The paper follows your units until you pick one yourself: metric caves start on metric paper, imperial caves on imperial.
- The page you see is exactly what **PNG** and **Print** produce.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The cave does not fit this paper at this scale: pick a bigger sheet or a smaller scale." | The map runs off the page at the chosen scale. | Choose a bigger paper, or a smaller scale such as **Scale: best fit**. |
| "No scale fits this cave on this paper." | Even the smallest scale is too large for this paper. | Choose a bigger paper. |
| "Could not open the print dialog." | The browser blocked the print window. | Allow pop-ups for CaveCAD, or use **PNG** instead. |
| The page is blank | No cave is open, or the engine is still loading. | Open a cave and wait a moment. |
