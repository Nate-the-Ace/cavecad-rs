# Rooms

The Rooms panel is where you choose who a cave is shared with, and whether each person can edit it or only watch.

## When you reach for it

- You own a cave and want a teammate to see or edit it.
- You want to change someone from editor to watcher, or take them off.
- You are a guest and want to check what seat you have.

![The Rooms panel](rooms.png)

## How to use it

1. Sign in at the top right and open one of your caves.
2. Open the Rooms panel. The top line shows the cave name, your part ("you own this cave", "you're an editor" or "you're watching"), and the sync state.
3. Press **Add a room**, type a name such as "Survey team", and press **Add**.
4. Pick the room in the list on the left. The **Private** room holds only the owners.
5. In the guest box, type a member's name, or anyone's email address.
6. Choose **Editor** or **Watcher** for the seat, then press **Add**.
7. Check the person under **Guests**.

### Change or remove a guest

1. Pick the room.
2. Change a guest's seat with the menu beside their name.
3. To take them off, press the cross beside their name.

### Rename or remove a room

1. Pick the room. The pencil renames it and **Remove room** deletes it.
2. Removing a room asks you to confirm first.

### Invite someone who is not a member yet

1. Type their email address and press **Add**. Only admins can invite this way.
2. They show as "invited, not joined yet" until they join.
3. If the email did not go, press **Copy link** and send the link yourself.
4. Press **Cancel** beside an invited person to take their seat back.

Editors can change the cave. Watchers can only look. Someone taken off keeps the copy already on their computer but gets no new edits. Only owners can change rooms.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Open a cave to see its rooms." | No cave is open. | Open a cave. |
| "Sign in (top right) to share this cave." | You are not signed in. | Sign in, then come back. |
| "This cave isn't kept on the server, so it can't be shared. Samples never are: make a new cave, or open one of your own." | The cave only lives on this computer. | Make a new cave, or open one of your own. |
| "Pick a member from the list, or type someone's email to invite them." | What you typed matched nobody. | Choose a name from the list, or type a full email address. |
| "Sign in to change rooms." | Your sign-in has lapsed. | Sign in again. |
| "Offline: your edits will join when you reconnect" | You have no connection. | Carry on. Your edits join when you are back online. |
| "Sync stopped: something didn't check out" | Sharing hit a problem. A reason may follow. | Press the refresh button at the top right of the panel. |
