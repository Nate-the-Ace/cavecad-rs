# Scans

The Scans panel holds your scanned notebook pages and sketches, and lets you link them to trips and line them up under the map.

## When you reach for it

- You have photos or PDFs of field-book pages and want them kept with the cave.
- You want a sketch to sit under the Map so you can trace over it.
- You want a trip's notebook page and its scan to stay together.

![The Scans panel](scans.png)

## How to use it

1. Open the **Scans** panel. It sits below the [Profile](profile).
2. Click **Add scans…** and pick a folder of pictures or PDFs. You can also drop files or a folder onto the panel. They are kept with the cave, so your other devices and anyone you share it with see them.
3. Pick a page in the tree on the left. Use **Find a scan** to search by name. Scroll to zoom and drag to pan, and use the **Fit** and **Turn** buttons above the page.
4. Pick the trip in the **Trip** list, then click **Link to trip**. The page now belongs to that trip and shows under **This trip's scans**.
5. To keep only the sketch, click **Trim to outline**. Drag a loop round the part to keep, or click its corners, then click **Close outline**.
6. Click **Align under map** to place the page. Click a spot on the scan, then click that spot's station on the Map, or choose it from the **Station…** list.
7. Match at least two spots. Two matches place and scale the scan, and more fit it better. Click **Done** when you finish.
8. Keep **Show under map** ticked to see the scan under the [Map](map). Use **Strength** to make it fainter or stronger.

### Place a scan in a profile or cross section

1. Link the scan to a trip first.
2. Open the **Place in** list and choose **Map**, **Profile** or **Cross Section**. Each has its own placement.
3. Click **Align under map** and match spots as above. In a Cross Section you can tick **Set scale from a wall** to use a measured wall.

### Fit style

With three or more matches, the **Fit** list appears. **Rigid** keeps the scan's shape, **Stretch** takes out scanner stretch, and **Rubber sheet** bends the scan so every match lands on its station.

### Track what you have done

- Right-click a page or folder and choose **Mark complete** or **Mark all complete**.
- Tick **Hide complete** to see only the pages that still need attention.
- Right-click and choose **Remove from cave…** to delete a page for everyone.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Sign in to add scans: they are kept with the cave on the CaveCAD server." | You are not signed in. | Sign in, then add scans again. |
| "You can look at this cave’s scans but not add them." | You are a watcher on this cave. | Ask the owner for edit access. |
| "This cave is not syncing, so scans cannot be added to it now." | The connection to the server is down. | Check your connection and try again. |
| "No scans (pictures or PDFs) were found there." | The folder you picked has no usable pictures or PDFs. | Pick a different folder. |
| "That outline is too small or not a shape." | The trim outline has too few corners or is too small. | Trace a larger outline of at least three corners. |
| "The survey has changed under this page since it was matched..." | Stations have moved since you lined the scan up. | Click **Check the matches**, or **It still lines up** if it is fine. |
| A message that there is no measurement at this station | The wall you picked has no measured distance. | Enter it in the [Notebook](notebook), or pick another wall. |
| "No scan matches." | Your search hides every page. | Clear **Find a scan**. |
