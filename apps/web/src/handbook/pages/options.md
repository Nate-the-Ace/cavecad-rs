# Options and the View tab

Settings that are not part of a running tool live in two places: the **View** tab of the ribbon, and **Options** in the window behind your round initial at the top right.

## The View tab

- **Panels** brings back a closed panel.
- **Units**: Metric or Imperial. This decides what every length shows and what you type.
- **Drawing scale**, **Map style**, **Rock halo** and **Map view** change how the map is drawn.
- **Layout** and **Saved layouts** arrange the panels.

## Options (your initial, top right)

- **Display**: dark, light or follow the system (**Auto**), and the highlight colour; **Lengths** (the same setting as the View tab's Units); **Compact ribbon**, which folds every ribbon group into a dropdown; and **Reset the panel layout**.
- **Symbols**: the symbol standard, US (NSS) or International (UIS), the same choice as the buttons at the top of the [Symbols](symbols) panel.

The options of a tool that is running are not here: they are in the ribbon tab that opens for the tool.
