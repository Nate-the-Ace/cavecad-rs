# Erasing

The Eraser cuts away the part of a line it passes over, and takes away whole fills, symbols, text and leaders it touches.

It works in the [Map](map), the [Profile](profile), the [Cross Section](section) and the sketch panes. A pen's eraser end does exactly the same, whichever tool is picked.

## Erase with the Eraser

1. On the Draw tab, in the **Erase** group, click **Eraser**.
2. Drag across what you want gone. A circle under the pointer shows how much the eraser takes.
3. Let go. A line keeps everything outside the circle's path; the pieces either side stay lines of the same type and style.
4. Click **Eraser** again, or press Escape, to put it down.

One drag is one step: Undo brings back everything that drag took.

## Erase with a pen

- Turn the pen over and rub with its eraser end. This works with any pen that has one (Wacom, Surface Pen, Windows Ink pens and most others) without picking the Eraser.
- A pen with no eraser end, such as the Samsung S Pen, can erase with its side button: pick **Eraser**, then tick **Pen side button erases**. While it is on, holding the side button as the pen touches erases instead of opening the right-click menu.
- An Apple Pencil has no eraser the app can see: pick **Eraser** and draw with the tip.

## Size

The Eraser's options tab has **Size**, in millimetres on the printed page. It erases the same width of the drawing at any zoom: zoom in to erase finely.

## What it takes

| What | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Walls, ledges, sketch lines and other lines | Only the part under the eraser goes. A closed line that is cut becomes an open one. |
| Fills (sand, water, breakdown and other areas) | The whole fill goes when the eraser enters it or touches its outline. |
| Symbols | The whole symbol goes when the eraser touches it. |
| Text and leaders | The whole text or leader goes when the eraser touches it. |

Things hidden with **Show** are never erased.
