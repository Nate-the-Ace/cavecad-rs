# Profile

The Profile shows one run of the cave from the side, so you can see how the passage rises and falls, and sketch on it.

## When you reach for it

- You want to see the cave's height changes along a run.
- You want to draw the side view: ceiling, floor, ledges and symbols.
- You want to place cross-section sketches next to their stations.

![The Profile panel](profile.png)

## How to use it

1. Open the **Run** list at the top and pick a run. Each entry gives its name, how many stations it has and its length.
2. Look at the picture. The solid lines are the survey legs. The dashed lines are the measured ceiling and floor.
3. Scroll to zoom. Drag on empty space to pan.
4. Click **Fit band (or double middle-click)** to frame the whole run.
5. Click a station's triangle to pick it. The ribbon then shows that station.
6. Choose a drawing tool in the ribbon, then draw with a pen or the mouse. A stroke becomes a smooth curve.
7. Click **Undo in the profile** if you make a mistake. Ctrl or Cmd with Z works too.

### Edit a line you drew

1. Click the line to select it. Its fit points appear.
2. Drag a point to move it.
3. Double-click on the curve to add a point.
4. Press Delete to remove the selected line.

### Cross sections beside stations

1. Tick **Sections** in the toolbar. Sketches made in the [Cross Section](section) panel appear beside their stations.
2. Drag one to place it. The drop is saved.

### Continue a line

When you start a stroke on the end of an open line of the same kind, it carries that line on. A ring shows where.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Open a cave, then pick a run." | No cave is open, or no run is chosen | Open a cave, then pick from the **Run** list |
| "The band stops at …: no usable connection beyond it." | The run could not be drawn past that station | Check the survey legs beyond that station |
| "N stations on side branches are not on this band." | Branches off the run are not drawn here | Pick the branch from the **Run** list |
| "N sketches hang on stations that are not on this band." | Some sketches belong to stations on another run | Pick the run that holds those stations |
| "A fill needs a loop: draw all the way around the area." | A filled area was not closed | Draw all the way round the area |
| Sketches next to stations are missing | **Sections** is not ticked | Tick **Sections** |
