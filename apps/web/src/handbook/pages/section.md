# Cross Section

The Cross Section shows the passage cut across at one station, with its measured walls, and is where you sketch the real shape.

## When you reach for it

- You want to draw a passage's cross section from the field notes.
- You want to see how the measured walls at a station line up.
- You want to move from station to station and draw each section in turn.

![The Cross Section panel](section.png)

## How to use it

1. Open the **Station** list at the top and pick a station. Or click a station on the [Map](map).
2. Use **Previous station** and **Next station** to step along the survey.
3. Look at the shaded area with a dashed edge. It is a guide to trace against, drawn from the walls measured there.
4. Choose a drawing tool in the ribbon and sketch the real shape on top.
5. Click **Fit (or double middle-click)** to frame the section. Scroll to zoom, and drag to pan.
6. Click **Undo in this cross section** if you make a mistake.
7. The arrow at bottom right says whether the picture's top is "up" or "north". The bar at bottom left gives the scale.

### Edit a sketch

1. Click a line to select it and show its fit points.
2. Drag a point to move it, or double-click the curve to add one.
3. Press Delete to remove the selected line.

### Stations with no walls

A station in the list marked **(no walls)** has no outline. You can still sketch there. Walls are recorded with the shot into a station, so the first station of a chain has none.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Open a cave with surveyed legs to cut cross sections at its stations." | The cave has no surveyed legs | Bring survey data in, or add shots in the [Notebook](notebook) |
| "Station … has no measured wall points of its own, so there is no outline to trace against." | Nothing was measured into this station | Sketch from your notes, or pick the next station |
| "Station … has only N measured wall directions; 3 are needed for an outline." | Too few wall readings for an outline | Sketch from your notes, or add readings in the [Notebook](notebook) |
| "(no walls)" beside a station in the list | That station has no outline | Sketch by hand, or pick another station |
| "A fill needs a loop: draw all the way around the area." | A filled area was not closed | Draw all the way round the area |
