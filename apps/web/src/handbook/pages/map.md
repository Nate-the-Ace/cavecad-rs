# Map

The Map shows the whole cave from above or any other side, with its passage walls, and is where you draw plan sketches.

## When you reach for it

- You want to see the cave as a whole and check the survey looks right.
- You want to sketch walls, symbols and notes on the plan.
- You want to pick a station or a shot and see its details.
- You want to hide trips or kinds of drawing to see one thing clearly.

![The Map panel](map.png)

## How to use it

1. Turn the view by dragging, or use the cube in the top right corner. Pan and zoom to look closer.
2. Click **Home view** to go back to the starting angle, or **Fit to cave** to frame everything.
3. Click **Show** to open the list of things you can hide or show. Tick **Walls**, **Passage**, **Sections** and **Profile sketches** as you like.
4. Tick **Adjusted** under **On the map** to see the survey with loop errors spread out, instead of as measured.
5. Click a station to pick it. The [Cross Section](section) panel opens at that station.
6. Click a leg or a splay to pick it and see its details.
7. To draw, choose a drawing tool first. Then click the TOP face of the cube and draw on the map.

### Hide a trip or a kind of drawing

1. Click **Show**.
2. Untick a name under **Types**, **Symbols** or **Trips**.
3. Click **Show everything** when you want it all back.

### Keep the view from turning

1. Click the padlock under the cube. Its tooltip says "Lock the view so it cannot be turned by accident while you sketch".
2. The view can no longer be turned. Pan and zoom still work.
3. Click the padlock again to unlock.

### Several things under one click

When more than one thing sits under the pointer, a list offers them all. The top one is already picked.

### Colour by height

Click the palette button, titled **Colour by elevation**, to colour the passage by its height.

### Point-snapping

Click **Snap** (or press F3) to snap your drawing to the ends, middles and crossings of what is already drawn.

## What can go wrong

| Message or symptom | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Snap to the Top view to draw on the map" | You chose a drawing tool, but the view is turned | Click the TOP face of the cube, then draw |
| "A fill needs a loop: draw all the way around the area." | A filled area was drawn without closing it | Draw all the way round and finish where you started |
| "N hidden drawing" button in the toolbar | Some drawings hang on shots that were deleted | Put the shot back, or click the button to delete them |
| "Fix N line" button in the toolbar | Some lines hop between passages on different levels | Click it to re-attach each line to the level it starts on |
| "Other level" button | A line starts where two passages overlap | Click it to move the line to the other level |
| "Drag a bigger circle: at least …" | The circle round the entrance was too small | Drag a larger circle round the entrance |
| Nothing shows, or the Show list is crowded | Types or trips are hidden | Click **Show**, then **Show everything** |
