# Capture field test: Sullivan Cave, 2026-11-21

## Before you lose signal
- Phone charged; gimbal charged; a light that can sit on or beside the rig (LiDAR needs light to track).
- The three cards: CURRENT, FORWARD, REAR (plus the laser target if you use it).
- Open Capture once with signal: Sullivan Cave must be in the list (it is kept on the phone already).
- Gimbal: Mimo app fully closed, phone Bluetooth on, phone portrait in the clamp.

## Once, before the first station
- CaveCAD: Map → Show → LiDAR rooms → **Link Capture shots…** → On My iPhone → Capture (if not already linked).

## At each station (A1, A2, …)
1. Survey the shot into Sullivan Cave in **CaveCAD** first (tape, compass, clino). Then in Capture → Sullivan Cave
   the station is in the list as "A1 · from CaveCAD on this phone": pick it. If you shoot before surveying it,
   use **Other station…** and type the name; the shot waits and drops into place when CaveCAD has the station.
2. Cards: **CURRENT** on this station's mark, **FORWARD** on the next station, **REAR** on the previous one.
   Prop them up facing the rig, lit, within about 4 m.
3. Gimbal: stand it over the mark, switch Gimbal on, press **Start**; let the sweep and fill finish (about
   2½ minutes, it chimes). By hand: **Start**, turn slowly round the room looking at each card, **Stop**.
4. The shot saves at its station (or "waiting for station" if typed). From the second station on, shots that saw
   their shared cards say "chained to …". Switch to CaveCAD → Map: the room shows on its station (LiDAR rooms).
5. Move on: CURRENT goes to the next station, the old CURRENT spot gets REAR, FORWARD moves ahead.

## Worth noting (a line each is plenty)
- Which stations you shot, and anything odd: a card not seen, "Compass unsure", "Too dark", the gimbal
  stopping early, the app closing.
- Roughly how far the cards were and whether any lay flat.

## Afterwards
- Back in signal, open Capture: the shots upload by themselves ("n shots, k to upload" counts down).
- Survey the stations into Sullivan Cave in CaveCAD as usual; once it is on the server, tap **Get latest**
  in Capture and the waiting shots drop into place.
- CaveCAD's Console (last tab on the phone) shows what the link did ("capture" lines).
- Tell Claude "read the log": the whole day's log is on the phone and is read over Wi-Fi at home.

The test builds on the phone are signed until 23 Nov (CaveCAD) and 27 Nov (Capture).
