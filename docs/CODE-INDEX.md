## Crates

- **cave-doc**: The in-memory cave: trips, stations and shots, edited only through stamped [`Action`]s.
- **cave-draw**: Authoring operations: what a drawing tool does, as document actions.
- **cave-dxf**: DXF export and import with a lossless round trip for the cave itself.
- **cave-fill**: Area fills: a closed outline filled with a pattern (sand, clay, breakdown, water, flowstone, ...).
- **cave-fixture**: Builds a [`Document`] from a simple shot-list JSON, so the app and tests have real caves to show before the file importers exist.
- **cave-geom**: 2D geometry for traced cave linework: points, paths with curves, boxes and moves.
- **cave-id**: Stable identifiers for everything the survey stores (stations, shots, entities, layers, trips).
- **cave-log**: Backend feedback for the read-only console: a bounded in-memory log every tool reports to.
- **cave-mesh**: The surveyed passage as a 3D surface, ported from the old `CsMesh3d`.
- **cave-notebook**: The survey notebook: the survey laid out the way a notes page is, and the edits that go with it.
- **cave-pattern**: Patterns people author: motifs (small drawings), linetypes (dashes, dots and motifs repeated along a line) and fills (hatches, dots and tiled motifs repeated inside an outline).
- **cave-profile**: The extended elevation: one survey run unrolled onto a single horizontal axis.
- **cave-scans**: Which scanned pages go with which trip.
- **cave-scene**: What the 3D canvas draws: stations, legs, splays and loop closures, in metres (x east, y north, z up, relative to the survey's anchor).
- **cave-shape**: Shaped lines: ornament generated along a drawn curve (ledge hachures, pit ticks, slope fans, flowstone and rimstone scallops, wall glyphs).
- **cave-sheet**: Sheets: a map on paper.
- **cave-shelf**: What the Cave Shelf shows about a cave without opening it: its name, how big it is, each trip's date, team, shots and where it started and stopped, and the open ends where a new trip could begin.
- **cave-store**: The storage interface every cave file goes through, plus an in-memory implementation.
- **cave-style**: The type palette: one table that says how every kind of thing looks.
- **cave-survey**: Survey math: shots to station positions, loop closure measurement, and least-squares adjustment.
- **cave-symbols**: The symbol catalogue: every point symbol a cartographer can place, with its names and its drawing.
- **cave-walls**: Passage walls from LRUD (left, right, up, down) measurements and splays.
- **cave-wasm**: The browser's door into the Rust engine: thin `wasm-bindgen` wrappers, no logic of their own.
