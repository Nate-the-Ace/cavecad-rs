Breathe. The bootloader can't be broken by a bad update.

It lives in its own flash sector, and no update erases or writes it. As long as the battery holds a charge, you can always get back to boot mode and flash a working firmware.

C. Reflash firmware

Flashing erases stored shots. The new image is first written into the shot-memory area. Get unsent shots off the X1 first if you can, and back up calibration coefficients with Cavway Assistant. It's not yet known whether calibration survives an update.

1

Connect the USB cable. It's steadier than Bluetooth, and the bootloader listens on both. On the Mac it appears as a CH343 serial port at 115200 baud.

ls /dev/cu.usbmodem*
2

Enter boot mode (B). The app won't take a firmware update. Only the bootloader will.

3

Flash with Cavway Assistant (Windows) or the Cavway Assistant web port. Pick a .bin that has Cavway's 256-byte header. The flasher sends three steps:

K · erase staging→L × n · 128-byte packets, CRC-checked→M · checksum, then copy into the app
4

Read the screen:

UPGRADING...

Copying into the app area. Takes a few seconds, so don't power off now.

UPGRADE SUCCESS

Done. Power off with CLR and start normally.

UPGRADE FAIL

The checksum didn't match, so the old app wasn't touched. Go to E.

E. After a failed or interrupted flash

  • "UPGRADE FAIL", a dropped link, or the flasher timed out before the end: the app area was never erased. Start the X1 normally, or retry from C.
  • Power cut during "UPGRADING...": the app may be half-copied and won't start. The bootloader is untouched: enter boot mode (B) and flash again.
  • Nothing at all when holding the arrows plus DIST: charge it for an hour and try again. If it's still dark, contact Cavway support.

Check it's alive

A read that changes nothing. Over USB it should answer 01 01 07 00 (the app version), and over Bluetooth use x1-peek.

cd ~/Documents/github/cavecad-rs-ble/tools/cave-x1 && ./run-mac.sh x1-peek E000:4 E004:4

Bluetooth must be on and the X1 must not be connected to the phone. Details and addresses are in tools/cave-x1/FIRMWARE.md.
